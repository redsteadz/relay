# Vision And Principles

Relay reduces notification, email, and SMS clutter without hiding consequential information.
Incoming data becomes a searchable quiet archive, concise actionable events, and user-controlled
automations.

## Principles

- Quiet by default: only useful or requested information interrupts.
- Explainable: category, extraction, filter, disclosure, and action decisions retain provenance.
- User-controlled: approval is default; automatic behavior is explicit per rule.
- Data-minimal: retain raw payloads briefly and disclose only required fields to AI/providers.
- Extensible: source and action providers share typed contracts without controlling domain logic.
- Reversible where possible: suggestions precede effects; irreversible dismissal gets stronger gates.

Relay is not a general surveillance tool, an advertising profile, or an autonomous agent allowed to
invent external actions.
